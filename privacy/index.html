Privacy Policy for Lingpal

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Lingpal is a language learning app that stores your vocabulary and stories on your device and can sync them through your private iCloud database. No Lingpal account or email address is required to use the app.

Information stored by the app

Lingpal stores the words you save, their translations and notes, generated stories, and your language preferences. An optional display name and appearance and reading preferences are stored locally on your device.

The developer does not receive your vocabulary, stories, display name, or language preferences. Lingpal does not use a third-party account system or database service.

On-device translation and AI

Translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework. AI word notes and story generation use Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework when Apple Intelligence is available. Lingpal does not send your words or stories to an external AI provider.

Downloading translation language packs or Apple Intelligence models may require an internet connection and is managed by Apple.

iCloud sync

When an iCloud account and iCloud services are available, Lingpal uses Apple's CloudKit service to sync saved vocabulary, stories, and language preferences with your private iCloud database. The developer cannot browse the contents of your private iCloud database.

iCloud is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. The app can store data locally when iCloud is unavailable.

Advertising and tracking

Lingpal does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Retention and deletion

Your saved content remains in the app until you delete it or remove the local app data. When iCloud sync is active, changes to synced records are also synced through iCloud. Removing the app from one device does not necessarily remove copies stored in iCloud or on other devices. You can manage app storage and iCloud storage through Apple's device settings.

Because the developer does not have access to your private app content, the developer cannot retrieve or delete that content on your behalf.

Support requests

If you email the developer, the developer receives the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive information or private vocabulary unless it is needed for your support request.

Children

Lingpal does not require an account and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you have a concern about information included in a support request, contact the developer.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Website hosting

The Lingpal privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details. This website does not include advertising or analytics scripts.

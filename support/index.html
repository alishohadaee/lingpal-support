Getting started

Lingpal requires iOS 26.2 or later and a compatible iPhone. You do not need to create a Lingpal account.

Translations

Download the languages you need in Settings → Apps → Translate → Languages. Return to Lingpal, tap Refresh during onboarding, and choose your native and learning languages in Profile.

Apple Intelligence

AI word notes and story generation require a compatible device, Apple Intelligence enabled in Settings, and downloaded on-device models. Availability also depends on your selected language and region.

iCloud sync

Vocabulary, stories, and language preferences sync through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. Check that your devices use the same Apple Account and have iCloud enabled. Lingpal can also store your content locally.
